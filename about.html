Welcome to Kita-Sora,
a small sea under the northern sky.

Under the northern sky there's a small sea. A little boat keeps sailing out, toward new shores, new people, new versions of herself, and keeps finding her way back. Exploring outward, settling inward. Every voyage ends at Ithaca, and every Ithaca becomes the start of the next one.

Kita-Sora means "the northern sky." It was named in a small snowy town in the north, the place where I first learned to stay.
