about Kita-Sora

A map made from the things I could not leave behind.

Kita-Sora is a small world for memories, movement, and the quiet work of becoming yourself again.

Kita-Sora began before it had a name. It began with photographs, handwritten notes, a postcard kept too long, and the small objects that made a life feel real when everything else was changing.

I kept noticing that these things were not random. They were footprints, each one leading back to a version of me who had been somewhere, wanted something, or learned how to stay. Together, they became a map.

So this is not a shop built to make you become someone else. It is a place to keep what helps you recognise yourself: letters from the road, pieces made by hand, and reminders that a life can move without losing its shape.

Some pages here are still only pencil lines. Some places are waiting for their next chapter. That is part of the map too.

I’m Lynn.

I write, make images, and collect the small evidence of living. Kita-Sora is where I place it all, slowly, so that anyone arriving in the middle of their own becoming has somewhere gentle to begin.

You do not have to arrive here in any particular way. You can simply stay for a while.
