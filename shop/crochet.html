Harbor Loop · Series 01 · The Keeper of Little Routines

Mugi

Mugi likes his days to have a rhythm.

He wakes up slowly, follows the same little paths, and goes fishing whenever the water feels right. Salmon happens to be his favorite — catching one is simply part of a very good day.

Mugi believes ordinary things become special when you return to them often enough. Different day, different fish, same way home.

Harbor Loop · Series 01 · The Little Collector

Piku

Piku has a talent for finding treasures nobody else notices.

Buttons, ribbons, tiny flowers, a very good pebble — if it feels important to her, it goes straight into the little pocket on her bib.

Nobody quite knows how much she keeps in there. Piku only knows one thing: there’s always room for one more.

Harbor Loop · Series 01 · The Little Pathfinder

Raku

Raku notices things before most people do.

A shift in the wind, a tiny trail in the grass, a sound somewhere behind the trees — his senses always seem to be turned up just a little higher.

He is brave, but exploration does not have to mean going somewhere dangerous. Sometimes the biggest discoveries are already close by, waiting for someone to look carefully enough.

Raku never leaves without his compass, a tiny magnifying glass, and a little backpack — just in case something worth following appears.
