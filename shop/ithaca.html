a postcard collection from Yakumo

six cards for a life in motion

To Ithaca began in a small northern town, with a question: what if arriving is simply another way to begin? These cards hold the departures, returns, and becoming that we meet more than once.

the story hidden in the set
The cards appear to move from departure to arrival. But life does not move in a straight line.
You might leave something behind, find yourself without a map, breathe again, become, arrive, and then begin all over again. Ithaca is not the end of this story. It is the gentle place where the next one begins.
